# Stage 2: Visual Direction

## Start when

The approved brief and Design Interpretation Note are complete.

## Designer task

Develop one or two genuinely different visual directions. Each direction must respond to the brief, use realistic content and contain a clear idea that can guide the full design system.

## Cowork prompt

```text
Help me develop two genuinely different visual directions for this client.

Read first:
- the approved client brief
- the Design Interpretation Note in Figma
- reference/02_DESIGN_QUALITY_STANDARD.md

Before proposing a direction, list the facts that are established and any information
that is still missing. Ask only questions that would materially change the design.

For each direction provide:
1. A short name
2. A one-sentence design thesis
3. The intended audience response
4. A signature element that supports the thesis
5. Colour roles and the purpose of each role, without treating exploratory values as final
6. Typography roles and the personality each role carries
7. Layout rhythm, density, shape and imagery approach
8. The homepage hero idea, one content pattern and the primary action
9. Concrete do and do not guidance
10. A rationale tied directly to the brief

The two directions must differ in concept, hierarchy and composition, not only in colour.
Avoid generic AI design patterns listed in the quality standard. Use realistic copy rather
than filler.

Show the written plan for both directions first and stop. After Designer review, help me
develop the selected directions into Figma concept frames. Do not treat exploratory
tokens or components as approved system values.

If the available Figma access cannot perform an approved action, give me the exact manual
Figma steps and wait while I complete them. Do not claim the action was completed.
```

## Review and approval

The Designer reviews and refines the directions before presentation. The client approves one direction in writing.

Record:

- approved direction name
- final design thesis
- signature element
- approval date and evidence
- requested refinements

Do not build the client design system until one direction is approved.
