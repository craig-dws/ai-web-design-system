# 1. Design the homepage in Claude Design

Paste this into Claude Design, with the client folder (brief, brand assets, photos) attached. It
designs the homepage from the brief; the next step transfers it to Claude Code to build the Figma
design system.

---

Design the **homepage** for this client from the brief. The brief already carries the strategy,
audience, voice and per-page notes, so do not re-do strategy, design to what it says.

**Read first:** the client brief (`website_design_brief.md` or equivalent), and the brand assets
(logo files, photos).

**Design to:**
- **The brand palette and fonts exactly**, with the roles the brief gives them. Modernise within the
  palette, do not invent a new identity. Keep the signature accent colour dominant.
- **The voice, made visual** (the brief's tone section): match the mood it describes.
- **The homepage notes** in the brief (the key-pages section): sections, hierarchy, the primary call
  to action in the header, the body and the foot, tap-to-call phone on mobile.
- **Avoid the AI look:** intentional asymmetry and spatial rhythm, brand-specific colour, an
  unexpected hierarchy. Not centred-and-symmetric with uniform padding. Honour the brief's "what to
  avoid" (for example no high-pressure sales design).
- **Accessibility, WCAG 2.2 AA:** check every text-on-background pair. Where a bright brand colour
  fails on white (a common trap), use it as a field or on a dark ground, not as text or a thin mark
  on white.

**Then:** design the homepage (desktop), and use **"transfer to Claude Code"** so we can build the
Figma design system from it (prompt 2).

Design against realistic content from the brief, not filler. British and Australian English, no em
dashes, no emojis unless the brand calls for them.
